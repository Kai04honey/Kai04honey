- Confirmed: the state of Leonida (Rockstar's Florida) with Vice City (Miami); NPCs filming on phones; in-game social-media feeds; the alligator wandering into a convenience store (trailer 1).

- Real precedents to cite: GTA V NPCs forget you instantly; RDR2 NPCs recognise and remember the player (honor system); GTA V's 5-star police escalation.
